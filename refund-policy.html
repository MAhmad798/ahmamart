Return, Refund & Cancellation

Cancellations: you can cancel your order any time before it's dispatched by contacting us with your order code.

Returns: if an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or not as described, let us know within 3 days of delivery with your order code and a photo of the item, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Refunds: since orders are paid in advance, approved refunds are processed via bank transfer or mobile wallet to the details you provide — we'll confirm the method with you directly.

Items must be unused and in their original packaging to be eligible for return, unless the issue is a defect or wrong item received.
