Online shopping in Pakistan with AhmaMart

AhmaMart is an online store based in Usmanwala, Kasur, Punjab, delivering everyday products across Pakistan — shoes, clothing, jewellery, gifts, toys, home decor, health essentials and electronics. Pick what you need, check out in a minute and we confirm and dispatch your order.

How you can pay

Pay in advance with JazzCash, EasyPaisa or SadaPay, or choose Cash on Delivery on small orders. The total is always shown before you confirm, and you can order as a guest without creating an account.

Delivery and returns

Most orders arrive within 2–5 business days. If an item is damaged or incorrect, tell us within 3 days of delivery and we will arrange a replacement or refund. Read our shipping policy and return, refund & cancellation policy, or find quick answers in the FAQ. You can also contact us on WhatsApp, phone or email.
